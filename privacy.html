Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Who we are

Wendygo Studio is an independent software publisher based in Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain. We build browser extensions for productivity and content creation. Contact: wendygostudio@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. Our extensions (TextForge, FrameForge, CLIForge) process all data entirely within your browser. No text, images, files, or personal information is sent to any server, collected, stored, or shared by us — ever.

Local storage

Our extensions may use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save your preferences (language, theme) and, if you activate a PRO license, your license key. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

License validation

If you purchase a PRO license, the extension contacts the Lemon Squeezy API (lemonsqueezy.com) to validate your license key. This request contains only your license key and an instance identifier — no personal data, no browsing history, no content from the extension. Lemon Squeezy acts as our Merchant of Record; their privacy policy governs the purchase transaction.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or telemetry in our extensions or on this website.

Third-party services

Our extensions do not integrate with any third-party services other than the Lemon Squeezy license API described above. They do not inject scripts into web pages, do not read your browsing history, and do not access any data outside the extension popup.

Permissions

TextForge and CLIForge request zero browser permissions beyond the default popup. FrameForge requests zero browser permissions. If you activate a PRO license, the extensions request host permission only for api.lemonsqueezy.com to validate the key.

Children's privacy

Our extensions are general-purpose tools and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Since we collect no data, changes are unlikely to be material.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at wendygostudio@gmail.com.
